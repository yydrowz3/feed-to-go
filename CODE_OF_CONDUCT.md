# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Feed to Go is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive community.
Everyone who participates is expected to help make the project a safe place to
learn, contribute, and collaborate, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat others with empathy, patience, and respect.
- Welcome newcomers and people with different backgrounds and perspectives.
- Give constructive feedback about ideas and work, and accept feedback graciously.
- Discuss technical disagreements on their merits without personal attacks.
- Respect personal boundaries, privacy, and requests to stop unwanted contact.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize when appropriate, and work to
  repair harm.
- Keep discussions relevant and contribute to the health of the community.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or encouragement of violence.
- Insults, derogatory remarks, trolling, or personal or political attacks directed
  at participants.
- Sexualized language or imagery, unwanted sexual attention, or sexual advances.
- Repeated unwanted contact, stalking, or deliberate disruption of discussions.
- Publishing or threatening to publish private information without explicit
  permission, including addresses, contact details, credentials, or private messages.
- Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern or participates in a review.
- Other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
  professional, collaborative community.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces, including the repository, issues, pull
requests, code reviews, discussions, documentation, and community channels or
events maintained by the project. It also applies when someone officially
represents Feed to Go in public, such as through an official account or as an
appointed representative at an event.

## Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact a project maintainer
privately using a contact method they have published on their repository-hosting
profile or in project documentation. If a report concerns a maintainer, contact
another maintainer when possible. If no suitable private contact is available,
use the hosting platform's abuse-reporting tools where applicable. Do not post
sensitive incident details in a public issue or discussion.

Include only information you are comfortable sharing, such as what happened,
when and where it occurred, relevant links or evidence, and any immediate
concerns. You do not need to confront the person involved before making a report.

Maintainers will handle reports discreetly and share information only with people
who need it to review or address the incident. Absolute confidentiality cannot
be guaranteed. Reports will be assessed fairly and in context, and anyone with
a conflict of interest should step aside from the review.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code. They may
remove, edit, or reject contributions and communications that violate it, and
will explain moderation decisions when appropriate without exposing private
information.

Depending on the severity, impact, and pattern of behavior, a response may include:

1. A private request to stop the behavior and guidance on expected conduct.
2. A formal warning with clear conditions for continued participation.
3. A temporary restriction or suspension from project spaces.
4. A permanent ban for severe or repeated violations.

These responses are guidelines, not a required sequence. Serious incidents may
result in immediate restrictions. The same standards apply to contributors,
maintainers, and other participants.

Anyone affected by an enforcement decision may request reconsideration privately
through the reporting channels above, providing relevant context or new evidence.
Where possible, a maintainer who was not involved in the original decision will
review the request.
